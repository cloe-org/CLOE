How to cite this package
========================

If you use CLOE, please cite this repository and the following papers:

- Theoretical Recipe (Cardone et al. 2025)

- Code Implementation (Joudaki et al. 2025)

- Inference and Forecasts (Canas-Herrera et al. 2025)

- Systematic Uncertainties (Blot et al. 2025)

- Validation and Performance (Martinelli et al. 2025)

- Additional papers by Euclid members (Crocce et al. 2025, Carrillo et al. 2025, Moretti et al. 2025, Goh et al. 2025, Legrand et al. 2025, Sakr/Fumagalli et al. 2025, Sciotti et al. 2025, Salvalaggio/Camacho et al. 2025, and others to be added)
