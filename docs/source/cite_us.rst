Citing CLOE
===========

CLOE is released by the Euclid Consortium. It is open source and available under terms consistent with our 
|link-to-license| based on LGPL (https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0).

.. note::

    If you use CLOE, please cite this repository and the following papers:

    - Theoretical Recipe (Euclid Collaboration: Cardone et al. 2026)

    - Code Implementation (Euclid Collaboration: Joudaki et al. 2026)

    - Inference and Forecasts (Euclid Collaboration: Cañas-Herrera et al. 2026)

    - Validation and Performance (Euclid Collaboration: Martinelli et al. 2026)

    - Extended Cosmologies (Euclid Collaboration: Goh et al. 2026)

    - Systematic Uncertainties (Euclid Collaboration: Blot et al. 2026)

    - Additional Euclid Collaboration papers by its members (Crocce et al. 2026, Carrillo et al. 2026, Moretti et al. 2026, Sakr/Fumagalli et al. 2026, Sciotti et al. 2026, Salvalaggio/Camacho et al. 2026, and others to be added)

In addition, please cite the associated papers and adhere to the corresponding licenses of codes that CLOE utilizes 
(such as ``CAMB``, ``CLASS``, ``HMCODE``, ``BACCO``, ``FAST-PT``, and others).
